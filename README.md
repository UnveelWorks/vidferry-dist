## Features

- **Works with the sites you use.** Paste a public link from YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, Vimeo, Twitch, and 1000+ more. The popular ones get a platform badge; the rest are still worth a try.
- **Pick your quality.** Choose Best, 1080p, 720p, or 480p, limited to what the source actually offers, and set a default you prefer.
- **Audio only.** Pull just the sound as an MP3 or M4A.
- **Clipboard auto-detect.** Copy a link before you open Vidferry and it is ready to go.
- **Several at once.** Queue downloads and choose how many run together.
- **Download history.** Reopen finished files or show them in their folder, and clear the list whenever you like.
- **Stuck on a bot check?** Optionally borrow cookies from Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Brave. Off unless you turn it on.
- **Private by default.** Vidferry runs on your device. No accounts, telemetry, or tracking. It only reaches the video site for the download itself and GitHub for updates.
